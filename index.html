Buy the hand tools first

These go in the bag before any power tool or socket set. Mark them the day they come out of the package.

  1. Klein D213-9NE, 9-inch side-cutting pliers. Daily cutter and grip. Buy these before anything else.
  2. Channellock 430X, 10-inch tongue-and-groove. Connectors, locks, anything the nines will not hold.
  3. Klein D507-12, 12-inch adjustable wrench. Hardware that is not a socket job.
  4. Klein 44200 cable-splicer knife. If it will not open with gloves, leave it.
  5. Headlamp plus spare batteries. Spare rides in the bag.
  6. Klein NCVT1P pocket voltage tester. Prove it on a known live source before a cabinet.

Stay with it

This is the other list. Not a tool. What I do when the day is already thin.

  1. Show up sober. The job does not care what yesterday was. Drinking ends the day before the truck does.
  2. Call before I decide. One person who already knows the story. A text is enough if the voice will not work.
  3. Eat and sleep like there is a call in the morning. A bad night is how a clean day gets stupid.
  4. Leave the site when the work is done. Overtime is not a reason to hang around a bad idea.
  5. One next honest move. Not a new life. The next thing I can still do today.
