For journeyman linemen who still clock in

The fear goes up the pole with you.

PTSD is not only a battlefield word. It lives in the truck at 5:12 a.m., in the yard before tailboard, and in the quiet second before you hook in to work that has already shown you what it can take.

This is the porch. The book is the field manual.

You do not need another safety video with stock footage of a sunset. You need language that sounds like the cab of a truck, and a sequence you can use when the body has already started the day without you.

Find Your Footing is a free self-help book written for journeyman linemen who earned the ticket and then felt the ground go sideways — after a flash, a funeral, a storm that would not end, or a stretch of mornings they could not name.

The ticket says you are qualified to work the line. It never said the line would not work on you.
