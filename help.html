Doors that actually open

Do not finish this shift alone.

A resource you will actually call beats a perfect list you will not. You do not have to explain line work first.

U.S. crisis support

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call, text, or chat. 24/7.
  • Crisis Text Line — text HELLO to 741741.
  • Veterans — 988, then press 1, or text 838255.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Free mental-health and addiction support

1-800-662-4357 — SAMHSA’s free, confidential treatment referral service, 24/7 in English and Spanish. Referred treatment may have costs.

1-800-985-5990 — Call or text for free, confidential disaster-related crisis counseling, 24/7.

More support options · Source: SAMHSA

Trade doors

  • IBEW halls and locals — many have peer support or know who to call.
  • CIASP (Construction Industry Alliance for Suicide Prevention) — construction-specific resources.
  • Construction Working Minds — peer support focused on the trades.

Ask your hall or safety department what they already run. Local is usually faster than national.

Recommended reading

  • The Body Keeps the Score — Bessel van der Kolk. How trauma lives in the body and brain. The one most people start with.
  • Complex PTSD: From Surviving to Thriving — Pete Walker. Practical, less clinical, useful for the long version.
