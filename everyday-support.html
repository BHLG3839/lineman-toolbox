01 / Your day

Make room for
yourself.

You don’t need to fix everything today. Start by noticing what you need, and choose one manageable step.

Check in before you push through.

Ask yourself: How am I sleeping? What am I carrying? Who could I talk to today? You don’t need to give yourself a score or put a label on it.

If fatigue or distress is affecting your ability to work safely, follow your workplace safety procedures and tell the appropriate person.

Build a small transition after work.

When you’re safely off the job, make space to eat, rest, take a quiet walk, or talk with someone you trust. Choose what works for you—not another task to get perfect.

Say it simply.

“I’ve been having a rough time. Can we talk for a few minutes?”
“I’m not looking for you to fix it. I just need someone to listen.”

Get support beyond self-care.

Small habits are not a substitute for care. If distress persists, worsens, or interferes with daily life, consider talking with a qualified mental-health professional or your primary-care clinician.
